# Data Attribution & Licensing — LegalJarvis

LegalJarvis relies on open-access Indian legal datasets and open benchmarks. We gratefully acknowledge the following data providers and creators:

- **Provider**: Dattam Labs, via the AWS Open Data Registry

- **Attribution**: "Indian Supreme Court Judgments, Dattam Labs, accessed 2026 from the AWS Open Data Registry (s3://indian-supreme-court-judgments)."

## 2. Indian High Court Judgments
- **Provider**: Dattam Labs, via the AWS Open Data Registry
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY-4.0)
- **Attribution**: "Indian High Court Judgments, Dattam Labs, accessed 2026 from the AWS Open Data Registry (s3://indian-high-court-judgments)."

## 3. IL-TUR: Benchmark for Indian Legal Text Understanding and Reasoning
- **Authors**: Joshi et al. (ACL 2024)
- **Reference**: https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.618 / https://exploration-lab.github.io/IL-TUR/
- **Tasks**: Prior Case Retrieval (PCR), Summarization (SUMM), Rhetorical Roles (RR), Legal Statute Identification (LSI).

## 4. India Code Statutes
- **Source**: Legislative Department, Ministry of Law and Justice / National Informatics Centre (NIC)
- **URL**: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/
- **Content**: Central Acts including the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (2023), Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (2023), and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (2023).

- **Authors**: Paul et al., Law-AI Group, IIT Kharagpur (ICAIL 2023)
