## 1. Overview & PRD Alignment

Task T-18 implements **Reviewer Tooling and Gold-Set Protocols** per PRD Part F and Part G (Deliverable 9):
- **Gold-Set Integrity & Zero Auto-Generation (Rule 1 & Rule 3):**
  - All gold reference answers must be produced by certified human legal reviewers (advocates/researchers), never generated by LLM recall.
  - `validate_gold_qa_item` enforces valid reviewer initials and explicitly rejects automated tags (`ai`, `gpt`, `bot`, `synthetic`, `llm`, `claude`, `gemini`).
  - Decisions must be dated >= 2023 to avoid pre-2023 pre-training contamination of base models.
- **Strict Disjointness Enforcement:**
  - Per PRD D8: *"No LJ-Gold item, and no document linked to one, may appear in any tuning set. A test asserts disjointness."*
  - `verify_gold_tuning_disjointness` enforces `gold_doc_ids.isdisjoint(tuning_doc_ids)` and identifies any overlapping document IDs.
- **Reviewer Labelling Endpoints & Data Export:**
  - `POST /v1/feedback/reviewer`: Authenticated endpoint (requires `reviewer` or `admin` role) capturing 1-5 quality ratings, overall labels (`supported`, `unsupported`, `hallucinated`, `incomplete`, `wrong_law`), per-claim grounding checklists, and critique notes.
  - `GET /v1/feedback/export?reviewer_only=true`: Exports all human-annotated expert labels in standard JSONL benchmark format for error analysis and metric calculation.
- **Reviewer Portal UI (`frontend/src/pages/Reviewer.tsx`):**
  - Dedicated Reviewer Portal allowing certified practitioners to inspect answers, toggle per-claim support, enter critique, and download exported label sets.
- **Annotation Guidelines:**
  - Authored `docs/eval/gold_guidelines.md` documenting stratified domain sampling, reference answer standards, 30+ adversarial question types, and dual-reviewer Cohen's kappa agreement protocol.
  - Status reported honestly: Tooling and protocols are complete and verified; human reviewer panel recruitment is in progress (Rule 4 / AS-4).
