# Task T-15 Progress Report: Statutes, Versioning, and IPC/BNS Concordance

## 1. Task Summary
- **Task ID:** T-15
- **Objective:** Ingest core Indian criminal and procedural statutes (BNS, BNSS, BSA, IPC, CrPC, Evidence Act), implement temporal statute versioning with statutory transition notices and savings clauses (Section 358 BNS / Section 531 BNSS / Section 170 BSA), implement strict non-fabrication concordance review workflow (Rule 3), and build reference extraction.
- **Prerequisites:** T-03 (Database schema), T-02 (India Code probe).

---

## 2. Key Modules Implemented
1. **Statute Versioning (`backend/app/statutes/versions.py`):**
   - Temporal logic pegged to official enactment date: 1 July 2024 (`CRIMINAL_LAW_TRANSITION_DATE`).
   - Determines operational legal status (`in_force`, `repealed`, `not_yet_in_force`) for any incident date.
   - Generates statutory transition advice quoting exact savings clauses:
     - Section 358(1) BNS (repeal of IPC 1860 with savings for pre-July 2024 acts).
     - Section 531(2)(a) BNSS (repeal of CrPC 1973 with savings for pending inquiries, trials, appeals).
     - Section 170 BSA (repeal of Evidence Act 1872).
2. **Official Data Loader (`backend/app/statutes/loader.py`):**
   - Seeds the 6 primary Central Codes with authentic Act numbers, years, Gazette of India URLs, and sections:
     - IPC 1860 (Act 45 of 1860, repealed 30 June 2024)
     - BNS 2023 (Act 45 of 2023, in force 1 July 2024)
     - CrPC 1973 (Act 2 of 1974, repealed 30 June 2024)
     - BNSS 2023 (Act 46 of 2023, in force 1 July 2024)
     - Evidence Act 1872 (Act 1 of 1872, repealed 30 June 2024)
     - BSA 2023 (Act 47 of 2023, in force 1 July 2024)
3. **Concordance & Review Tooling (`backend/app/statutes/concordance.py`):**
   - **Strict Non-Fabrication Rule 3:** Concordance rows are never generated by LLM recall.
   - Every row strictly requires:
     - `source_url`: Official Gazette or Parliamentary committee comparative table.
     - `reviewed_by`: Initials / Identifier of legal reviewer.
     - `reviewed_at`: Certification date.
   - When no verified mapping exists, the system explicitly reports:
     *"No verified concordance mapping has been officially certified by a legal reviewer for this section. Under Rule 3, LegalJarvis does not guess or invent mappings."*
4. **Reference Extractor (`backend/app/statutes/references.py`):**
   - Extracts statutory provisions cited in judicial texts and creates `statute_references` records.
5. **API Router (`backend/app/api/statutes.py`):**
   - `GET /v1/statutes`: Lists all statutes, current status, and versions.
   - `GET /v1/statutes/lookup`: Looks up provision, transition notice, and certified concordance.
   - `POST /v1/statutes/concordance/import`: Reviewer certification endpoint.
6. **Documentation (`docs/data/statute_sources.md`):**
   - Comprehensive provenance of all 6 statutes, gazette URLs, and review protocols.
