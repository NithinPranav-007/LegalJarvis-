## Segmentation Architecture
1. **Numbered Paragraph Detection**: Matches Indian court judgment conventions (`1.`, `2.`, `[1]`, `(1)`, `Para 1.`, `Paragraph 1:`).
2. **Heading & Segment Typing**: Rule-based categorization into:
   - `facts`: Factual background, brief facts, case of the prosecution/appellant.
   - `issues`: Issues for consideration, questions of law, points for determination.
   - `arguments`: Submissions/contentions of learned counsel for appellant/respondent.
   - `reasoning`: Court's analysis, statutory interpretation, legal reasoning.
   - `holding`: Operative order, decree, conclusion, appeal allowed/dismissed.
   - `other`: Connective text and unclassified sections.
3. **Strict Invariant**: Enforces `text[chunk.start_char:chunk.end_char] == chunk.text` for 100% of generated chunks with zero character slippage.
